# Surface brief: ADP Toiture SA landing page

Scope: `src/routes/+page.svelte`, single static French page. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action

Geneva homeowners, property managers and construction firms deciding whether to trust a local roofer. Action: request a quote (mailto) or call 078 760 96 36.

## Content and proof

Copy and section order from the supplied comp: hero, services (4), company, projects (4), reviews (3), partners (5), footer. Photography is placeholder artwork until real photos are supplied. No proof beyond what the comp contains.

## Constraints

Static, no backend. WCAG AA. Eyebrow labels from the comp are folded into headings (craft floor bans them).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A trade firm's page that looks like the work: a hard-edged, sheet-metal register in near-black and signal red, photography first, refusing the soft-card SaaS arrangement.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black slate ground (#13171d), signal red (#d1111c) used for the one action and one emphasised word per band, white sheets for content, hairline steel greys; Hanken Grotesk semibold headings at tight tracking, square 4px corners like cut metal, photographs bleeding to the edges.
STORY: The visitor sees a Geneva roofer with a real workshop and team, understands the four trades, trusts the named references, and asks for a devis.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width header (logo left, five anchors, outlined phone button, solid red devis button right) over a dark hero: left 45% a three-line headline with "toiture." in red, a two-line promise, red devis button plus outlined "Nos services"; right 55% a roofing photograph dissolving into the slate at its left edge.
FORM: Directly from the supplied comp, code-led; seed key none (pinned by the user, no roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
